    Curation

    The judge's ledger: what it protected, what it flagged for downscaling, and the rules it has learned from your debates. Sections load when opened; keep several open at once.

    Protected titles

    Titles Curatarr won't suggest deleting, and why. Lift a protection to have it judged again on the next scan.

    Downscale candidates

    Kept, but bitrate outliers — reclaim storage without deleting. Click "Done" after transcoding.

    Upgrade candidates

    The inverse of downscale: titles you demonstrably love sitting on weak files (below 1080p or a lean bitrate). Acting on it is your click in the arr — nothing is re-grabbed automatically.

    Redundant storage

    Duplicate versions of the same title (4K Remux + 1080p) and the same id kept as separate items — the GB you could reclaim without losing anything.

    Learned principles

    Rules the curator extracted from your debates. Shadow = captured but not yet applied; activate to inject it into the judge. contradiction = your call.

    Wrap-Up

    Your period in review: what you watched and heard, when, what you defended and what you let go — told by the curator that argued with you about it.

    Stop server Stops Curatarr

    Stops Curatarr gracefully: running jobs finish their current step first. Start it again on the server to come back.

    Hide history for removed media

    Hides watch history for titles that are no longer in any Plex library from history, taste and recommendations. The plays are kept for your Wrap-Up, and come back if the title returns to Plex. Spotify history is never hidden. Nothing is hidden if a library can't be read.
